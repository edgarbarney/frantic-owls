# The Owl Post: A Guide to Letter Writing

*Frantic's Wizarding Communication. Licenced by the British Ministry of Magic.*

This guide is for owlries that run **immersive mode**: no enchanted slab, only quill, parchment and owls. If your server hands you a phone instead, read [PLAYER_GUIDE.md](PLAYER_GUIDE.md).

**Pure-bloods:** You know how this works. Write, seal, send.

**Muggle-borns:** It is e-mail, but slower and far more dignified. Your letters arrive in your inbox, the ones you wrote wait in your sent letters, and every letter is written by hand on a sheet of parchment.

Type `/owl` to sit down at your writing desk, or use the up-arrow key on your keyboard. Your owlery may also have **owl posts** around the world: Walk up to one and press the key it shows.

## Terms

| Wizarding term | What muggles call it | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Letter** / **Owl** | E-mail | A letter written on parchment. Both words mean the same thing here. |
| **Letters** | Inbox | Every letter an owl has brought you, newest first. A red wax seal means unread. |
| **Sent** | Sent folder | Every letter you wrote, with who it went to. |
| **Correspondents** | Contacts | Everyone you have written to. Whoever you write to joins your correspondents; writing to someone does not make you one of theirs. |
| **Owl post** | Post office | A place in the world where letters can be read and written, on owlries that require one. |
| **Official letter** | Automated e-mail | A letter from the Ministry or another official sender. Its sender's name is on it, and it usually cannot be answered. |

## Your Desk

The desk opens in the middle of your screen and dims the world behind it. At the top you see:

- **Letters** and **Sent**: Your two piles. A number beside Letters counts the unread ones.
- **Write a Letter**: A fresh sheet of parchment.
- **The gear**: Settings.
- **The cross** (or Escape): Put your quill down and leave the desk.

On owlries with owl posts, a little label at the top tells you whether you are standing at one.

## Reading a Letter

Tap a letter to break its seal. It appears exactly as its writer wrote it: The same handwriting, the same lines, the same gaps, on every witch's and wizard's screen.

You are never told who sent a letter. You learn it the way a muggle would, from the letter itself: Most writers sign at the bottom, and your Letters list shows that signature for you. A writer who does not sign stays anonymous, so read unsigned letters with care. Official letters are the exception and carry their sender's name.

**Answer** writes back to whoever sent the letter, even an anonymous one. Your greeting addresses them by the name they signed with.

## Writing a Letter

1. **Dear...** Start typing a name right after "Dear" and pick from the list: Witches and wizards **online now**, or **your correspondents**. Add as many as the owlery allows. Click a name to cross it out again.
2. **The body.** Write what you like in between. Spaces, indents and empty lines are kept exactly.
3. **Sincerely...** Your sign-off and name are filled in for you.
4. **Press the wax seal** to send it.

Writing to several people does not make a group: Each of them receives their own copy of the same letter, addressed to everyone you named, and answers only to you.

**The quill** (Edit Whole Letter) turns your letter into one plain sheet with the greeting and signature written out, so you can change "Dear" and "Sincerely" to whatever suits you, or leave your name off and send it **unsigned**. **Back to Template** undoes those edits (click it twice).

Half-written letters wait for you: Leave the desk and your letter is still on the sheet when you come back. **Discard** (click it twice) throws one away.

## Owl Posts

Some owlries only let you handle letters at an owl post:

- **Anywhere**: Read and write wherever you are. Nothing to worry about.
- **Writing only at a post**: You can read anywhere, but must visit an owl post to write.
- **Reading only at a post**: Sealed letters show a padlock until you visit a post.
- **New letters anywhere, old ones at a post**: An unread letter can be opened wherever you are, but once read, you must visit a post to read it again.

The label at the top of your desk and the line above your letters tell you which rules your owlry uses.

## Handy Charms

- **Notices** (Settings): A slip of parchment drops in at the top of your screen when an owl arrives.
- **Arrival sound** (Settings): Pick the hoot, chime or quill scratch you like, and its volume.
- **Do Not Disturb** (Settings): Silence every hoot and notice. Letters still arrive. It can also switch on by itself during duels.
- **Language** (Settings): Your desk speaks the languages your owlry offers.

*Not every owlery offers every service. If something here is missing, your server has turned it off.*
