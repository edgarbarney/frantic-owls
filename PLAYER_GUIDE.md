# The Owl Post: A Guide for Witches and Wizards

*Frantic's Wizarding Communication. Licenced by the British Ministry of Magic.*

**Pure-bloods:** The enchanted slab in your hand is very similar to a device that muggles call a *mobile phone*. They carry one everywhere and talk through it across any distance. Do not be alarmed. Ours runs on owls.

**Muggle-borns:** It is basically a phone. Nearly everything will feel familiar once you know what we call things here.

Type `/owl` to take it out of your robes, or use up-arrow key on your keyboard.

## Terms

| Wizarding term | What muggles call it | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Owl** | Text message | A written message. It takes a moment to fly; "In flight…" means it has not landed yet. |
| **Your owl** | Phone number | Your character's own address. Each character has one, and it stays with them. |
| **Owl number** | The digits of a phone number | The 8 digits of your owl. Where the server shows them, they are on your card in Settings, and others can type them in to owl you, call you or save you. |
| **Bulk Owl** | Group chat | One owl sent to several contacts at once, who can all answer. Admins keep it in order. |
| **Floo-Call** | Phone call | Talk to a contact by voice, through the Floo instead of a wire or radio. |
| **Inform** | Swapping numbers | Hand your owl to a wizard standing near you so they can add you to their contacts. |
| **Official Owl** | Automated message | An owl from the Ministry or another official sender. It cannot be answered, only muted. |
| **Unsend** | Delete for everyone | Call back an owl you sent in the last few minutes. Everyone sees "This owl was unsent." instead. |
| **Location owl** | Sharing your location | An owl carrying the spot where you stand. **Show on map** marks it and draws a gold route there, which stays until you arrive or tap **Hide from map**. |
| **No longer flies** | Number disconnected | That character is gone. You may still read your old owls with them. |
| **Muggle Phone** / **Parchment** | Themes | Your enchanted slab can look as it is, or can look like a muggle phone. |

## Taking It Out

Your slab locks itself whenever you put it away. On the Muggle Phone, touch the **fingerprint** under the clock. On Parchment it arrives as a sealed letter: **Break the wax seal** to open it. You can turn the lock off under Appearance in Settings.

## The Apps

Your home screen holds five apps with these icons:

- 🔥 **Floo-Calls**: The call log and new calls. Missed calls show as a badge.
- 📖 **Contacts**: Every wizard whose owl you hold. Star the dearest ones as **Favourites**. Where the server allows it, the plus adds someone by their owl number, a contact's page lets you change their number, and **Send New Owl** and **New Floo-Call** take a number too. One number, one contact: Edit or delete the old one first.
- 🦉 **Owls**: All your chats. The badge counts owls you have not read yet.
- 📜 **Inform**: Offer your owl to someone nearby, or answer someone offering theirs (**Accept**, **Exchange** to give yours back, or **Decline**).
- ⚙️ **Settings**: Your picture, theme, house colours, sounds, ringtone, language, privacy and blocked owls.

## During a Floo-Call

- **The flame** (think *Incendio*) lights the Floo: It starts a call, or answers one ringing for you.
- **The snowflake** (that is *Glacius*) puts the fire out: It declines a call, or what muggles call "hanging up".
- **The microphone** mutes you. Struck through means nobody can hear you.

## Inside a Chat

- **Tap an owl** to **Reply**, **Forward** it to other chats, **Pin** it under the chat's header, **Unsend** it or **Delete for yourself**.
- **The magnifying glass** searches the whole chat, not only what is on screen. Tap a result to fly back to it.
- **The smiling face** opens the emoji picker. Muggles invented them; we added a Magic page.
- **The paperclip** opens what an owl can carry:
  - **Galleons** and **Items & gear** (one-to-one owls only) pack a parcel for your next owl. It leaves your pockets when you send, and the other side taps **Collect** once the owl lands. Anything nobody collects flies back to you after a few days, and unsending the owl brings it back at once. Neither of you is ever told who the other is.
  - **Location** sends a location owl.
  - **Poll** (bulk owls only) asks everyone a question to vote on. Tap an answer to vote, and tap it again to take your vote back.
- **Unfinished owls wait for you**: Leave a chat mid-sentence and the words stay in the box, marked **Draft** in your chat list.
- **The bell** mutes a chat. Owls still arrive; they just stop hooting.
- **Read** and **typing…** show when the other side has read your owl or is writing. Turn either off in Settings and you stop seeing theirs too. Fair is fair.

## Handy Charms

- **Phone sounds** (Settings, Notifications) are the little taps and clicks your slab makes as you use it. Turn them down or off there.
- **Do Not Disturb** (Settings) silences every hoot, ring and pop-up. It switches on by itself during duels if you let it.
- **Show original names** (Settings, Appearance) adds a contact's own name in parentheses after the name you saved them as, such as "Nat (Natsai Onai)". Only for wizards you know: someone you saved from a number or a chat stays as you named them.
- **Move & Resize Phone** (Settings, if your server allows it) lets you put the phone anywhere on screen. Lost it? `/owl_resetpos` summons it back.

*Not every owlery offers every service. If an app or button is missing, your server has turned it off.*
